# Agadir Driver SEO quick start

Reviewed 28 October 2026. The video methods were inspected from public post captions and sampled on-screen text. The first video's research/template approach was adapted to the existing React site. No platform migration or advertising account was needed. The second video's proxy-reload traffic tactic was excluded.

## Intent and page plan

The map covers existing real services. Volumes, organic difficulty and current positions are unknown; topics are grounded intent assignments, not volume-validated opportunities. The target is qualified demand and confirmed rides, not a guaranteed first place.

- `/`: **Agadir Driver**. Intent: branded navigation. Status: existing.
- `/agadir-airport-transfer`: **Agadir airport transfer**. Intent: airport transfer booking. Status: existing; taxi alias consolidated.
- `/agadir-taxi`: **Agadir taxi**. Intent: local private ride booking. Status: existing.
- `/private-driver-agadir`: **private driver Agadir**. Intent: hourly driver hire. Status: existing.

## Implemented

- One page-specific title, description, Open Graph URL/title/description and Twitter title/description per preferred page. The previous service/guide pages pointed social URL metadata at the homepage and reused the homepage description/title.
- `/agadir-airport-taxi` permanently redirects to `/agadir-airport-transfer`, retaining query parameters. The two were overlapping booking intents. Removed the alias from the sitemap and related-service links.
- Five useful service pages retain the existing booking form and gain distinct arrival, vehicle, local-ride or hourly-itinerary guidance.
- Published one-way prices are rendered from the existing tariff data, including the price guide. No tariff, vehicle capacity or business contact was changed.
- Visible breadcrumbs and Service/WebPage/BreadcrumbList structured data on service pages; Article structure and contextual service links on existing guides. Structured data makes no rating, availability or invented date claims.
- Missing blog slugs return a genuine 404 instead of an indexable generic guide response. Request pages retain noindex.
- Regression checks inspect the rendered production HTML, preferred URLs, schema JSON, alias redirect and unknown-article response.

## What the baseline does and does not establish

The initial bounded audit fetched all 11 URLs in the existing public sitemap. They returned page content and had titles, a single H1, canonicals and parseable business JSON-LD. Ten lacked a page-specific Open Graph title and used the homepage as their Open Graph URL; inherited descriptions and Twitter titles were also inconsistent. These metadata observations do not establish an actual Google ranking penalty.

No verified Search Console property, Keyword Planner data, analytics conversion baseline or Google Business Profile connection was available. Current positions, impressions, clicks, index coverage, query volumes, organic difficulty and AI citations remain unknown. A `site:` lookup or search API order was not treated as a ranking report. HTTP 200 is not proof of indexing. Request duration was not treated as Core Web Vitals.

The audit checks JSON syntax and visible-data consistency; no Google rich-result eligibility guarantee is made. Google currently says its AI features need no special markup or AI text file. FAQs remain useful, but the May 2026 FAQ rich-result deprecation means they are not being sold as a search-display trick.
