## Implementation order

1. Verify the confirmed Google account in Search Console and submit the current sitemap. Inspect actual indexing before calling the site indexed.
2. Preserve existing working English service pages and their stable URLs; improve the airport, Taghazout and Tamraght answers first. Use the existing price guide for price-intent queries.
3. After approval, publish genuine French versions at the proposed separate URLs, rendered in French on the server with localized titles, descriptions, canonicals, visible copy, structured data and reciprocal language links. A local-storage language toggle alone does not provide this.
4. Build only distinct pages justified by the chosen intent. The homepage remains an overview; airport and route pages contain deeper information. Do not create a page for each synonym or a hotel-name-only search.
5. Add the published French URLs to the sitemap, verify all internal links, and validate the rendered markup.
6. Inspect submitted pages in Search Console, record query/page/country/device baselines, then assess indexing after two weeks and rankings after four to six weeks. Use real received enquiries and confirmed bookings to measure business outcomes.

## Deferred / excluded

The full 216-row review is in keyword_research.csv, with exclusions and reasons. Competitor navigation, accommodation-only queries, public/shared taxi searches and unrelated terms are not commercial page targets. The 0–10 range is not zero; low-demand candidate phrases are deferred. Longer intercity routes need a separate supported content/tariff pass. French Tamraght is not promoted on an unproven high-volume claim.
