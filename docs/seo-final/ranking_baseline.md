## Keyword demand

The signed-in Keyword Planner returned 127 UK rows and 89 France rows for the configured discovery seeds. All 216 rows were captured, with market, language, network, period and source. Of these, 101 were mapped to useful intents, 87 excluded and 28 deferred. Fifteen primary targets were approved by the owner.

Monthly figures are ranges for October 2025–September 2026. Close variants can share estimates, so their ranges must not be summed as independent demand. Advertising competition is recorded separately and is not organic difficulty.
